About this page

This page is a fresh build by Visually Inconvenienced Hams, inspired by HamClock, the shack clock created by Elwood Downey, WB0OEW, who became a silent key on 29 January 2026. It uses no HamClock code. Live data is fetched in your browser from NOAA’s Space Weather Prediction Center, the DX Summit cluster, Parks on the Air, and CelesTrak. Nothing you do here is sent anywhere except those feed requests.
